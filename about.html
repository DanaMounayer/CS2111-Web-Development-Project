About us

On The Go introduction: who we are and why we built Jeddah's drive-thru guide.

What we offer

Whether you're on a road trip or just looking for a quick bite, we make it easy to find the perfect spot. Explore a list of drive-thru locations with their menus and prices, all designed for a seamless experience while you're on the go.

Why choose us?

Our list is made of drive-thru places loved by all ages, especially teenagers. We know how much fun it is to go for a car ride, so we feature the coolest spots that cater to your cravings, from classic favourites to trendy new options.

Why the drive-thru experience?

Drive-thrus have surged in popularity because they fit how we live now: quick, comfortable and contact-free. You can grab great food and drinks without parking, queuing or leaving your car.
